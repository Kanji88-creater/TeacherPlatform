Teacher Platform

Privacy

What is stored

Your account (name, email, role, department, school), the classes, pupils, marks, attendance and plans you enter, the documents you submit for checking, and optional pupil photos. A record is kept of who signed in, submitted, checked or viewed a document.

Who can see what

RoleCan seeCannot see
TeacherOnly their own classes, pupils, marks, plans and photos, and the check results on their own documents.Any other teacher's data.
HODDocuments that teachers in their own department submit, and numbers for their department.Other departments. Anything a teacher has not submitted. Photos.
Deputy HeadDepartment batches that HODs send, and school-wide numbers.Anything not sent in a batch. Photos.
Head teacherSchool-wide results, attendance rates, staff list, and how many documents are checked.Pupil names, photos and the contents of documents.
DeveloperSchemes of work uploaded for all teachers, and the number of schools and users.Not available to teachers' data through the app.

The person who hosts the server can technically reach the database, so the server must be kept secure, use HTTPS, and be backed up.

Pupil photos

Photos are optional. Only add a photo with the permission of the school and the pupil's parent or guardian. Photos are private to the teacher who added them and are never shown to other staff.

Protection

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Sign-in sessions use secure cookies. Every request is checked against your role on the server, not only in the app. Stamps carry a code that anyone can verify, and the check reveals only the document title and who checked it.

Your choices

In My account you can download all your data, remove it from your device, or delete your account and everything saved on the server.

This page describes how the app works. It is not legal advice. Schools should confirm their obligations under Zambia's data protection law.
